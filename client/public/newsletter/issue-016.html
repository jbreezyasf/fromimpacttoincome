① Main Story

Kre Dropped a Cinematic Video Masterclass and Left the Group with a Weapon

Kre (@mindovamillonairemom) taught the co-building session Wednesday night and gave the group a complete system for creating consistent, professional AI-generated video — and people were already shipping clips before the call ended.

AI video generation is everywhere right now, but most people are getting inconsistent results — characters changing faces mid-clip, random background noise baked into the footage, scenes that look like three different movies stitched together. Kre's session on Wednesday night addressed exactly that. She walked the group through how to use ChatGPT to build your prompt, then send it to a video generator like DaVinci to render the actual footage.

Donna Kendrick Spivey had a cinematic luxury brand clip live before the session was even over. First attempt. Kre gave her a real-time tip in the chat: make sure your prompt tells the generator to keep the character and scene consistent, and strip all background audio so you can add music and voiceover in post. Then Kre dropped the full Master Video Consistency prompt in the group — character continuity, environment continuity, cinematic color grading, clean audio, the whole thing. It's one of the most useful raw prompts shared in this group all month.

Here's where people get stuck: they treat the video generator like it should read their mind. It won't. If you don't explicitly tell it not to change the character's face, it will. If you don't tell it no background voices, you'll get a clip with random crowd noise baked in that you can't remove. The prompt isn't optional — it's the whole job. Kre also pointed out that if a generator mispronounces a name, you go back into the prompt and spell it phonetically, then explain to the GPT why you wrote it that way so the next prompt carries that context forward.

  • Use ChatGPT to build the prompt, DaVinci to render: Don't try to prompt the video generator directly from scratch. Build your scene description in ChatGPT first, then pass the finished prompt to DaVinci or your preferred video tool.
  • Consistency is a prompt instruction, not a default: Explicitly tell the generator to maintain the same character face, clothing, environment, and lighting throughout. If you don't say it, it won't do it.
  • Generate clean audio, add everything in post: Tell the generator no background voices, no music, no sound effects. It's nearly impossible to remove baked-in audio. Add your own music, voiceover, and sound design during editing.
💼 How does this apply to your business?

If you're doing brand content, product promos, or client-facing marketing videos, this workflow is a cheat code. Imagine a real estate agent generating a cinematic walkthrough teaser for a new listing, or a boutique brand running luxury product clips without a film crew. Donna was already applying it to a luxury brand concept the same night she learned it. The system is in the group — go use it.
