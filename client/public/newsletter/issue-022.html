③ Worth Knowing

Build for Accessibility Now — Not After a Lawsuit

Juanita dropped a practical warning this week that applies to every consumer-facing app in the group: make sure your site is WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliant. WCAG stands for Web Content Accessibility Guidelines — these are the standards that determine whether blind and low-vision users can actually use your product with tools like Apple VoiceOver and Android TalkBack. The legal exposure is real. There have been lawsuits, and if you're building a financial app, a home care app, or anything the public touches, this is not optional for long. Cheryl added that if your app is public-facing in the EU, you also need to comply with the EU AI Act, which requires visible labels on any chatbot, deepfake content, or AI-generated material.

Natasha Brown had already shared the WCAG prompt in the group earlier this year and re-dropped it: 'Ensure this site and associated pages meets WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, specifically checking for color contrast and ARIA labels.' Add that to your guardrails on every build from the jump. Britt mentioned she already has text-to-speech built into her platform and flagged Spanish translation as another untapped market hiding in plain sight. Anthony said it best: this should be in the early stages of the build, not a retrofit.

⑤ Quick Hits
01
Use Claude Code to Save Lovable Credits

Multiple members this week hit the Lovable credit wall mid-build and had to stop. Rodney Williams, Natasha, and Kre all use the same workaround: build through Claude code and push changes to Lovable instead of prompting inside Lovable directly. It saves a significant number of credits and gives you more control. If Lovable has you in a chokehold, this is the unlock.

02
Own Your ISBN — Don't Let Amazon Own Your Book

Anthony Washington Sr published his book this week using his own ISBN purchased through Bowker. The difference matters: Amazon's ISBN keeps you on Amazon's terms. Your own ISBN lets you control the publisher name, the distribution channels, and your publishing identity. If you're building a brand around your content, this is a $125 decision that pays off forever.

03
TrustMRR.com — See What SaaS Is Actually Making Money

Cheryl Tibbs found a site that shows real revenue from SaaS companies that have connected their Stripe accounts. Her point was sharp: don't look at what people say is hot, look at what people are actually paying for. One of the top earners on the site is a basic website chatbot company. Derrick's response? He's literally building a chatbot for Credit System IO right now. Study the model, the niche, the pricing, and the simplicity of the offer — then find where you can apply it to a market you already understand.

04
OpenAI Is Cutting Cursor Off in November

OpenAI announced it will end access to its models on Cursor, citing concerns that SpaceX won't use the technology within its terms of service. The cutoff date is November 12, and OpenAI says it will not provide future models to Cursor going forward. Juanita flagged that this will likely affect members who learned to build using that method. If Cursor is in your workflow, start planning your migration now.
