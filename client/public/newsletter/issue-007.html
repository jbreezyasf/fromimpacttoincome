① Main Story

Cheryl Tibbs Earned the Claude API Certification — Here's What That Actually Means

Not a participation trophy — 8 hours of video, 10 quizzes, 3 weeks of work, and proof you understand what's happening under the hood when you build with Claude.

Anthropic has a certification track inside their Skilljar platform, and most people don't even know it exists. The one Cheryl completed — Claude with the Anthropic API — is the main cert for builders who want to prove they know Claude beyond just chatting in the interface. We're talking API calls, how the model processes requests, what's actually happening when you send a prompt. It's the knowledge that separates people who use Claude from people who build with it.

Cheryl dropped the link directly in the group: anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-with-the-anthropic-api. She didn't sugarcoat it. Eight hours of video, ten quizzes, roughly three weeks start to finish. She said, 'Not gonna lie, this one made me earn it.' Some people in the group do study sessions and knock out the quizzes together. She went solo. Both approaches work — pick what fits your life.

Here's where people get confused: there is a separate Claude Architect Certification on the same platform, but that one is reserved for official Claude Partners. This cert is not that. What Cheryl completed is the big one that's open to anyone serious about building. If you've been skipping this because it looked hard, that's exactly why you should go do it.

  • It Lives on Skilljar: Go to anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-with-the-anthropic-api — it's publicly accessible. You don't need to be a Claude Partner to take it.
  • The Architect Cert Is Different: Anthropic has about 15 certs total. The Architect track is partner-only. This is the next tier down — and it's still the one serious builders are going after.
  • Plan for 3 Weeks: 8 hours of video plus 10 quizzes. Don't schedule this for a weekend and expect to be done. Budget real time or find a study partner in the group.
💼 How does this apply to your business?

If you're selling AI services to businesses and your client asks why they should trust you over someone who just discovered ChatGPT last month, this cert is part of your answer. An AI consultant with a Claude API certification walking into a discovery call with a mid-size company hits different than someone showing up with vibes and a portfolio. It's the credential that says you understand the infrastructure, not just the interface.

That's how you run multiple projects without a runaway infrastructure bill. Some features are behind the paywall — keep that in mind — but for builds in the early stages, this approach keeps your overhead low while you're getting to revenue.

California's New AI Disclosure Law Landed in the Chat — and the Group Had Questions

Coach shared an urgent reel about new AI disclosure requirements dropping in California, and it immediately sparked a real conversation about what it means for people marketing AI-generated content and running businesses in that state.

This isn't just a California problem. As AI-generated images, videos, and copy become standard in marketing, disclosure laws are going to follow. Social platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok already have encryption that detects AI-generated content. The question the group raised — does the disclosure have to be verbal in the content, or is a label in the description enough — is one every AI content creator is going to need a clear answer to. Get ahead of it now.
