① Main Story

Tab Just Went Paid — And The Group Found Out the Hard Way

Tab's free tier hit a daily usage limit this week and started dropping upsell messages mid-project, and the AI Junkies were the first ones to feel it.

Tab is the AI personal assistant several members have been using to make videos, draft content, send emails, and even place phone calls. Up until this week, it felt pretty unlimited. Then, mid-episode, mid-workflow — it stopped. The app sent a message: daily usage limit hit, paused until midnight. For some members it explained the new pricing tiers. For others it just went silent.

Anthony got the hard version — Tab stopped responding to text entirely. Althea got the detailed breakdown: Free, $20/month Fast, and $200/month Express plans, plus a one-time $10 boost. Kre emailed them and got one reply confirming the rollout. Sherry Carter said she'd been asking for an upsell option anyway because Tab was the only thing getting her videos right. Multiple members hit the wall on the same day, which made it feel coordinated — because it was.

Here's where people got twisted: some assumed it was a bug or that they broke something. It wasn't. Tab ran a beta, let power users go deep, and now they're monetizing. Kre clocked it first: 'They sounding like the ppl we talked about last night on the call. UPSELL.' The real question for the group now is whether the $20/month tier holds up under heavy daily use — because Kre paid it and still hit the limit.

  • Free tier resets at midnight: Once you hit the daily allowance, Tab slows down or pauses until midnight Eastern. Everything you sent is saved — it picks it up at reset.
  • Three tiers are now live: Free (daily limit), Fast at $20/month (bigger allowance, background work keeps running), and Express at $200/month (first in line, largest allowance). There's also a $10 one-time boost.
  • The $20 plan isn't bulletproof: Kre paid the $20 upgrade and still hit a daily cap. If you're running heavy video workflows or long episodes, watch your usage even on paid.

💼 How does this apply to your business?

If you're using Tab to produce client deliverables — video content, email drafts, call summaries — you cannot afford to hit a wall at 2pm on a Tuesday. Factor the $20/month into your service pricing as a hard cost, same as any other tool. If your client pays you $500 for content, $20 for reliable throughput is not a debate.

Instinct Got Group Chats and Tab's Phone Voice Is European

Two quick things the group surfaced this week that are worth knowing. First: Instinct launched group chats for iMessage. You can add Instinct to any iMessage group, it deploys a Group Instinct for everyone in that thread, and it works even for people who don't have Instinct personally. Anthony shared the announcement straight from the app and said he was thinking about testing it. The catch: Instinct still can't make phone calls. She told it to change the voice and not sound robotic. It did. She liked it after. Kre followed up and confirmed she was able to improve hers too. Kanekalon Kindle, who used Tab's calling feature for a client, said it sounds 'a little robotic but very clear' and that the client was impressed. Resend launched dedicated email inboxes for AI agents. You get 3 inboxes free; additional inboxes are $1 each. If you're building agents that send or receive email, this is worth knowing. Anthony dropped the link late Wednesday night and Kre was already on it.
