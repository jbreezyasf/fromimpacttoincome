④ Hot Topic
🔥 From the Group Chat

Premortems: The Planning Session Nobody Wants to Do

Big Steele introduced premortems during Tuesday's Expert Office Hours and it sparked real conversation about how AI projects fail differently than normal software.

The premortem reframe — 'we already failed, why?' — forces you to name the landmines before you step on them. For AI builds specifically, the failure modes are sneakier: prompts that work on test cases but break on edge cases, costs that balloon from uncapped token usage, model updates that shift behavior, users who stop trusting outputs.

Big Steele
"Most of these are obvious in hindsight and invisible in planning. The premortem reframe gets people to name them out loud instead of assuming someone else is handling it."
Big Steele
"Run it after your prototype works but before you wire it into production. Thirty minutes, whole team, write silently first then share."
Dom Cortez
"If it makes you want to give up, take a step back walk around tackle it from another angle. Just dont give up!"
