① Main Story

Stop Paying for Software You Could Own: The AI Software Playbook Is Here

Derrick, Big Steele, and LaSean spent months building a step-by-step system for turning your app ideas into real software — and Junkies got first access this week.

The AI Software Playbook is a structured, 12-part, 134-step guide that walks you through planning, building, launching, and growing software — no prior coding experience required. It includes 348 copy-paste prompts, 169 fill-in templates, and blueprints for 14 types of apps including booking systems, CRMs, client portals, and mobile apps. There's also a practice business built in so you can learn the process before applying it to your own idea. The price for Junkies is $297 one-time with code EARLY at wealthpioneer.io.

Derrick, Big Steele, and LaSean were meeting at 5 AM every morning to build this thing. That's not marketing copy — that's the actual origin story. They took their real client work, their real mistakes, and their real process and organized it into something you can follow click by click. Nathan jumped in after the Thursday call and said it's 'really thorough and covers everything we've learned and more.' That's from someone who's been in the trenches with the group.

Here's where people get it twisted: they think they need to know how to code first. You don't. The playbook is clear — AI writes the code, your job is knowing what to ask for, in what order, and how to check the result. The mistake most people make is jumping into a build without a process, which is why they get stuck or end up with something that half-works. This is the process, documented from actual builds.

  • You Don't Need to Code: AI writes the code. Your job is knowing what to ask for and in what sequence. The playbook teaches exactly that, including what to do when something breaks.
  • Own What You Build: As Big Steele put it this week: the app is now the cheapest part to build. Stop renting tools and start owning your data and what processes it.
  • The AI Chief of Staff Path: Beyond building your own app, the playbook includes a path to build an AI Chief of Staff that answers questions from your business files, handles tasks, and gives you a morning brief.
