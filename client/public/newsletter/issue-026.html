① Main Story

The Motion Video Takeover: How One X Post Turned the Whole Group Into Content Studios

Britt shared a motion design workflow from X and within 48 hours half the group was generating promo videos for their apps, products, and personal brands — for free.

Motion graphics used to require a designer, a budget, and time you don't have. This week that changed for the AI Junkies. Britt dropped a link to an X post showing how to feed a design brief into Claude Code to generate animated motion videos — no After Effects, no Premiere, no freelancer. Just a prompt and a runtime.

The chain reaction was immediate. Tearra Spencer was up all night making videos. Anthony built a screen-breakout video for his athletic recruiting launch using a detailed prompt in Gemini then refined it in ChatGPT. Michelle R dropped a promo for the Signature Delivery Suite. Natasha updated her personal brand visuals. Althea used GPT 6 Astra to generate three promo options for Wisdom Talks and then had Instinct's AI agent Maya pick the best one. Juanita extended her content production hub. Britt woke up Tuesday morning to 12 reels Claude Code had generated while she walked the dog. The group challenge was simple: get your marketing video done before the month ends.

Where people get stuck is the prompt. Most members want to start vague — 'make me a promo video' — and then wonder why it looks generic. Anthony's detailed video prompt for his flag football athlete sequence showed exactly what works: define the subject, the scene, the action beat by beat, the audio, and explicitly list what to avoid. That level of specificity is the difference between a video you post and one you delete. Also worth noting: Claude Code is not required. Althea proved GPT 6 Astra gets you there too if you don't have access yet.

  • The Prompt Is the Brief: Treat your video prompt like a creative brief to a director. Scene, subject, action sequence, audio, and a 'do not do' list. Anthony's 400-word prompt for the flag football video is the template to copy.
  • Claude Code as Overnight Producer: Britt let Claude Code run autonomous reel generation while she slept. Woke up to 12 reels. Let the machine work the night shift while you rest.
  • Veed's Open-Edit Repo + Motion Video Kit: Britt dropped two GitHub repos — github.com/veedstudio/open-edit and the motion-video-kit — that extend what you can build. Feed them to Claude Code and let it sort through what complements your current stack.

⑤ Quick Hits
01
Use 'Slice' for Large Projects in Instinct

Anthony dropped this tip mid-week and it's practical gold. When you're running a large project in Instinct — he was working with about 80,000 records — tell it to 'slice' instead of letting it run the whole thing at once. You stay in control, you see what's happening step by step, and nothing goes sideways without you knowing.

02
Opus 5.5 Creates Videos Natively — No MCP Required
