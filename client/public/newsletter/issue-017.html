① Main Story

Big Steele Built a Bot That Joins Your Zoom Calls and Reports Back

After months of building, a custom AI agent is now joining live Zoom sessions, recording everything, and routing it back to the right client in a RAG system — and it's not using Cloudflare Stream.

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Think of it as giving your AI a personal filing cabinet — instead of guessing, it pulls from documents, transcripts, and session notes you've actually fed it. Big Steele built an agent that joins a Zoom call like a participant, listens, and then takes everything it captured — video, chat, audio — and automatically assigns it to the correct client inside his system. No manual sorting. No copy-paste.

The group had been watching this thing get built in pieces for weeks. When Big Steele posted 'It's alive!' on Sunday evening, the reactions flooded in — 10 of them on that message alone. He followed up with the context: 'Been working on this for months and finally got it joining zoom. Working on the video, chat, and audio recording so it goes back to my RAG and assigns it the right client.' He also confirmed he wired a voice into it so the agent can talk to him during a Zoom session. Juanita immediately asked if he used Cloudflare Stream. He didn't — too expensive at scale.

Here's where people get tripped up: they think you need one big AI tool that does everything. Big Steele's setup is the opposite. It's modular — a bot that joins calls, a recording pipeline, a RAG system, and an admin portal that surfaces behavior patterns, all talking to each other. He's also building self-learning feedback loops so his admin portal shows not just what users do in the app but why. That's the kind of infrastructure most people don't think about until they're already drowning in client data.

  • The Bot Joins Zoom Like a Human: It's not a third-party integration or a Zoom app — Big Steele's agent joins as a participant and handles video, chat, and audio capture independently.
  • RAG Does the Sorting: After the call, the recording doesn't just sit in a folder. The system processes it and assigns it to the right client profile automatically.
  • Cloudflare Stream Gets Expensive Fast: Big Steele ruled it out at scale. If you're building video infrastructure into a multi-client app, factor in streaming costs before you're locked in.
💼 How does this apply to your business?

If you run any kind of coaching program, consulting practice, or client-facing service with recurring calls, this architecture matters. Imagine every session auto-transcribed, tagged to the right client, and searchable from your dashboard — no VA needed. That's a real product you could build and sell to other coaches right now.
