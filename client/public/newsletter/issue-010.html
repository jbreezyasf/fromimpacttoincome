① Main Story

Anthony's Website Audit Play Is a Real Business in a Box

Running a Lovable audit, dropping it into Claude, and turning it into a recurring maintenance offer is one of the cleanest service models this group has broken down.

Here is the basic move. You build a site in Lovable. Before you hand it off or do anything else, you run the built-in audit. Lovable generates a report with issues, opportunities, and recommendations across SEO, performance, mobile responsiveness, and more. That report is your raw material. You take it to Claude, have Claude shape it into a professional, reusable audit template with whatever categories matter to your clients, and now you have a deliverable that looks like something a $10,000 agency would hand you.

Anthony laid the whole thing out in the chat Sunday and the reaction was immediate. Ten fire reactions and a thread that kept going. Britt connected it to her SEO, GEO, and AEO work and said it becomes a full add-on service once the process is locked in. Kre called it a golden ticket. Anthony pushed it further — what if the template was built so you just plug in the data and there is no prompt needed at all? That is the productized version. That is how you scale it.

Where people get stuck is thinking they have to build the website to make money on the website. Anthony said it plainly: sometimes the opportunity is not building, it is maintaining. A $49 a month basic maintenance package is a real offer you can make to any small business owner right now. Run the audit. Present the findings. Offer the plan. Monitor monthly. That loop does not require you to be an expert developer. It requires you to know how to use the tools you already have access to.

  • Lovable's SEO Feature Is New: Anthony noted that Lovable recently added a built-in SEO toggle. Run the audit after you enable it. Britt confirmed she was already working it. Check your project settings if you have not seen it yet.
  • Submit Your Sitemap to Google Search Console: Britt dropped this as her Sunday tip and Anthony co-signed it hard. Lovable historically did not index well. Submitting your sitemap manually and indexing your URLs fixes that. Do this on every build you ship.
  • Productize Before You Pitch: The version of this that scales is a template so dialed in that there is no prompt — just input the client info and it runs. Anthony and Kre both landed on that. Build it once, sell it forever.
💼 How does this apply to your business?

You just finished a Lovable site for a local accounting firm. Before you invoice, run the audit, let Claude turn it into a branded report, and walk back in with a $79 a month maintenance plan that covers SEO monitoring, uptime, and quarterly improvement recommendations. That accounting firm has four other business owner friends. One audit becomes a referral pipeline.
