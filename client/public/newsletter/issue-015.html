① Main Story

Big Steele Built a Living Memory System Because He Got Tired of Forgetting What People Told Him

A late-night build turned into one of the most practical AI ideas this group has ever discussed — and it might change how you think about relationships, CRMs, and what your AI should actually be doing.

Big Steele dropped a thought in the chat on Tuesday night that stopped everything: what if you never forgot what anyone in your life was trying to achieve? Not in a journaling way. In a real, automated, always-updated way. He wasn't asking for discussion. He'd already built it. The app pulls from your iMessages, Telegram, email, and call recordings, then builds a living profile on every person in your life — where they're trying to go, what's in the way, what you talked about last time, and what you could do this week to help them move forward.

Nathan was the first to see it clearly. He said it out loud: 'Whatever we think we can create, we can create. We don't even need certain software subscriptions anymore. We can use Claude and build our own tailored versions.' Britt agreed and said she was already 'phasing out tools and reallocating to marketing.' The conversation evolved fast. Nathan called it building an AI brain — a central system where everything you've learned, read, built, and recorded lives, and your agents can pull from it as a skill set. Big Steele described the architecture: a CRM meets Notion meets Obsidian, with a knowledge graph that nodes everything. 'A vision board and KPI insanity matrix,' his words.

Here's where people get stuck with this concept: they think about it as a smarter to-do list or a fancier CRM. It's not. The real insight is that the data already exists — it's just scattered and dead. Every goal someone shared with you is sitting in a message you'll never scroll back to. The tool isn't creating new information. It's resurrecting what was already there and making it actionable. Big Steele said he's loaded it with so many security scripts he's not sure it'll ever leave his own app. But the idea? That's already out.

  • The data already exists: Every goal, dream, and update people have shared with you is buried in your messages. The system doesn't create new info — it surfaces what's already there and makes it work for you.
  • Your AI brain is the foundation: Nathan and Britt are both building centralized AI systems where their knowledge, notes, processes, and agent skills all live in one place. Less subscription chaos, more leverage.
  • Build from your own frustration: Big Steele built this because he got tired of losing track of what people were working toward. Your best product ideas live in your own daily annoyances. Build the thing you actually need.
💼 How does this apply to your business?

Think about a coach, consultant, or nonprofit director managing 30 to 50 relationships at once. Every client has a goal they mentioned in passing, a milestone they're working toward, a blocker they shared on a call three weeks ago. This system makes that accessible in seconds instead of never. That's a real product for a real market — and Big Steele already proved it can be built in one night.

ChatGPT Image Hack: 3 at Once

Britt figured out that toggling on Intelligence in ChatGPT — hit the plus sign in the menu — lets you generate three images at once. Small toggle, big time saver if you're doing any volume of image work for clients or content.

Gemini Animation Prompt That Works
